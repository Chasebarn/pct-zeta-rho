How they got there
Each alum takes a few minutes on the same thing: what they studied, the first job they took, and what the two years after graduation actually looked like. No slides.

The rest of the night is questions. Recruiting timelines, internships, pay, grad school, switching majors late. Ask the one you actually care about, not the polite one.
