Resume structure
The order a recruiter actually reads in, what belongs in a bullet, and what to cut. Bring the version you have now, however rough it is.
LinkedIn
Headline, About, and experience. Those three fields decide whether a recruiter opens your profile or scrolls past it, and all three can be fixed in one sitting.
Talking about yourself
“Tell me about yourself” is a skill, not a personality trait. You will draft an answer and say it out loud to a member who has used one in a real recruiting conversation.
