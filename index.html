What a semester actually looks like.

Members run these projects rather than just attend them: planning fundraisers, leading volunteer days, and reporting what gets raised. Recent partners include the Hillsborough Education Foundation, JA Finance Park, and Parkland Cares. For most members it is their first real leadership role.

Guest speakers from industry, resume and interview workshops, and a structured new-member development program. Fall Summit brings the chapter together for a full day of professional training. The work is practical: the same skills recruiters test in interviews.

Intramural teams, Fall Formal, Spring Retreat, and chapter traditions through the semester. This is where members actually get to know each other, which is what makes the professional side work: people prepare, refer, and vouch for the members they know well.

Phi Chi ThetaZeta Rho · USF

Phi Chi Theta’s Zeta Rho Chapter was established at the University of South Florida in September 2018 by 18 founding business students. Since its founding, the chapter has focused on preparing members for internships and full-time roles through structured professional development, service, and chapter standards. Today, PCT continues to grow as a professional organization that balances career outcomes, philanthropy, and an active chapter culture.
