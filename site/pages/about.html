Zeta Rho at USF

Eighteen business students founded Phi Chi Theta’s USF chapter in September 2018.

Members host the Fall Summit, lead career workshops and volunteer with the Hillsborough Education Foundation. They also play intramurals, hold a fall formal and take a spring retreat.

Phi Chi Theta nationwide

Six women founded Phi Chi Theta in Chicago on June 16, 1924. Today, the fraternity connects business and economics students at 46 collegiate chapters with alumni across the country. USF belongs to its Central-Southern Region.

Members can attend regional conferences, serve in national leadership roles and apply for foundation scholarships.
