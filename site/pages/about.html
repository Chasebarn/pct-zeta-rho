From Chicago to USF

  1. 1924

    Phi Chi Theta is founded

    Six women from two business fraternities met in Chicago and founded Phi Chi Theta to advance business education.

    National history
  2. 2018

    Zeta Rho opens at USF

    Eighteen USF business students started the chapter. In their first year, they organized career workshops and visits to Raymond James and Tech Data.

  3. 2026

    The chapter today

    With 80+ active members and 500+ alumni, Zeta Rho brings students and local business leaders together for networking dinners, career workshops and the Fall Summit.

Service in Tampa Bay

Members organize fundraisers on campus, volunteer with groups like Feeding Tampa Bay and Habitat for Humanity, and lead service projects with their new-member classes.

$10,000+ raised for local causes

In our first year, we raised more than $2,000 for the American Cancer Society through Relay For Life. A new-member class later raised more than $1,400 for Hurricane Ian relief through United Way.

  • Community Food Pantry

    Members volunteer at the pantry, which provides groceries to Tampa-area families.

  • Pie a Phi Chi

    Members took pies in the face on campus to raise money for Moffitt Cancer Center.

  • A Kid’s Place

    The fall 2025 new-member class raised more than $1,000 for A Kid’s Place, which keeps siblings together in foster care.

25 Under 25

Each year, USF’s Muma College of Business selects 25 business students under 25, including undergraduates and graduate students from across USF’s campuses.

Applicants submit a résumé and personal statement showing what they’ve achieved in at least two of four areas.

  • Academics
  • Leadership
  • Professional development
  • Campus or community service
