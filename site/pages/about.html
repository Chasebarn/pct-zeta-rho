Phi Chi Theta at USF

From Chicago to USF

  1. 1924

    Six founders in Chicago

    Six women formed Phi Chi Theta to expand access to business education and connect students starting their careers.

  2. 2018

    Students start Zeta Rho

    Eighteen USF business students chartered Zeta Rho. In their first year, they visited Raymond James and Tech Data and raised more than $2,000 for the American Cancer Society.

  3. Today

    Still student-run

    80+ active members and 500+ alumni. Members organize career workshops, company visits and service projects across Tampa Bay.

Service in Tampa Bay

Members run campus fundraisers and volunteer with Feeding Tampa Bay and Habitat for Humanity. New-member classes lead their own service projects.

$10,000+ More than $10,000 raised for local causes Raised for local causes

  • Community Food Pantry

    Members volunteer at the pantry, which provides groceries to Tampa-area families.

  • Pie a Phi Chi

    Members took pies in the face on campus to raise money for Moffitt Cancer Center.

  • A Kid’s Place

    The fall 2025 new-member class raised more than $1,000 for A Kid’s Place, which keeps siblings together in foster care.
