Our history

Chicago, 1924

Six women from two business fraternities founded Phi Chi Theta on June 16, 1924. They wanted people preparing for careers in business to learn together and support one another.

Today, members across 46 collegiate chapters meet at regional conferences and can apply for Phi Chi Theta Foundation scholarships.

Tampa, 2018

Eighteen USF business students founded Zeta Rho in September 2018. Their chapter gave classmates a place to prepare together for internships and their first jobs.

Members now host industry speakers at the Fall Summit, lead new-member workshops and spend time together at intramurals, fall formal and spring retreat.

Service in Tampa

Members volunteer at the Community Food Pantry and raise money for Moffitt Cancer Center through Pie a Phi Chi, a campus fundraiser. The chapter has also supported Hillsborough Education Foundation, JA Finance Park and Parkland Cares.
