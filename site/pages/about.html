Meet the chapter at Rush

Our chapter at USF

Eighteen business students founded Zeta Rho at USF in September 2018. Members now run the Fall Summit, organize career workshops for new members, and volunteer with groups such as the Hillsborough Education Foundation.

Members also get together for intramural games, fall formal and spring retreat.

Phi Chi Theta

Part of a national network

Six women founded Phi Chi Theta in Chicago on June 16, 1924. Today, the professional business and economics fraternity connects students and alumni across the country.

Zeta Rho belongs to the Central-Southern Region. The national directory lists 46 collegiate chapters, alongside alumni chapters.

Members can pursue national leadership opportunities, attend regional conferences and apply for scholarships through the Phi Chi Theta Educational Foundation.

Faculty advisor

Carrington Price Jr.

When Zeta Rho’s founding students were organizing the chapter, Price was a graduate assistant in USF’s Bulls Business Community. He became their faculty advisor and continues to mentor members and advise the executive board.
