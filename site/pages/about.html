From Chicago to USF

  1. 1924

    Phi Chi Theta is founded

    Six women from two business fraternities met in Chicago and founded Phi Chi Theta to advance business education.

    National history
  2. 2018

    Zeta Rho comes to USF

    Eighteen business students founded the USF chapter. In their first year, they held career workshops and visited Raymond James and Tech Data.

  3. 2026

    The chapter today

    Zeta Rho is still student-run, with 80+ active members and a network of 500+ alumni.

25 Under 25

Each year, USF’s Muma College of Business recognizes 25 undergraduate and graduate business students under 25.

Applicants submit a résumé and personal statement. Muma looks for achievement in at least two of four areas covering academics, leadership, professional development, and campus or community service.
