Rush form privacy

What you share

The interest form asks for your name, email and permission to send recruitment updates. Your major or minor and graduation year are optional. We record your recruitment cycle and when you gave consent.

Who can see it

Your submission travels over an encrypted connection to the chapter’s Neon database. The account owners who manage the site and recruitment can access it. A hashed connection identifier helps limit spam.

Changes and removal

To correct your details or leave the list, message the chapter on Instagram at @pctusf.
