Your interest form details

How Zeta Rho uses the information you submit.

What the form saves

The form saves your name, email address, optional major or minor and graduation year, recruitment cycle, and your agreement to be contacted about Rush.

The chapter can use these details to contact you about recruitment. Submitting the form records interest in Rush and does not commit you to membership.

The website stores these details in the chapter’s Neon database. They are accessible to the account owners who manage the website and recruitment.

To request a correction or deletion, contact the chapter through @pctusf.

The form uses a hashed request identifier to limit spam. Your details are sent over an encrypted connection.
