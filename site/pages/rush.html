Rush PCT

Meet the students who run Phi Chi Theta at USF. Through Rush, you can talk to alumni about their first jobs and spend time with the chapter before you decide whether to apply.

See how Rush works

Who can rush?

Rush is open to business and economics majors and minors at USF, from first-year students to seniors. No previous fraternity or professional experience is required.

What to expect

Five of last fall’s six Rush events were open to students. The final event was by invitation.

  1. Meet the chapter

    At Info Night, meet the recruitment team and ask members how they fit PCT around classes.

  2. Workshop & alumni panel

    See how members build a résumé and introduce themselves. Then ask alumni how they found internships and first jobs after USF.

  3. Apply by invitation

    Selected students can apply for membership at the final event. If you receive a bid, the chapter is inviting you into the pledge program.

What should I wear?

Check the announcement for each event’s dress code.

Business casual
Wear a collared shirt, sweater or blouse with dress pants, khakis or a skirt. A dress also works. Choose dress shoes or flats.
Business professional
Wear a matching pantsuit or skirt suit, a solid-color dress shirt and closed-toe dress shoes.
What if I have to miss an event?

Attend as many events as you can. If you have a conflict, let the VP of Recruitment know early. You can reach the chapter at @pctusf.

Can I also be in a social fraternity or sorority?

Yes. Members can participate in both PCT and social Greek life at USF.
