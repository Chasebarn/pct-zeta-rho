Before you go

What should I wear?

Check the dress code on each event announcement.

Business casual
Wear a collared shirt, sweater or blouse with dress pants, khakis or a skirt. A dress also works. Choose dress shoes or flats.
Business professional
Wear a matching pantsuit or skirt suit, a solid-color dress shirt and closed-toe dress shoes.
What if I have to miss an event?

Come to as many events as you can. If you have a conflict, let the VP of Recruitment know as early as possible. Message the chapter at @pctusf.

Can I also be in a social fraternity or sorority?

Yes. Members can participate in both PCT and social Greek life at USF.

How much are dues?

Ask the recruitment team at Info Night for this semester’s dues and payment options.

Applications and bids

The chapter invites selected students to a final event, where they can complete the membership application. A bid is an invitation to the pledge program, which prepares candidates for initiation.
