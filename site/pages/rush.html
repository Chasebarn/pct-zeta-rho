What Rush Is

Rush is a six-event introduction to Phi Chi Theta at USF.

Meet current members, practice professional skills, and get a feel for the chapter before deciding if it’s a good fit.

Who Can Rush

  • Business or economicsOpen to students who major or minor in either field.
  • Any undergraduate yearOpen to current USF bachelor’s students in any class year.
  • No experience requiredYou don’t need prior fraternity or professional experience.

What You Gain

Résumé and LinkedIn

Practice describing your experience in résumé bullets and on your LinkedIn profile.

Professional Connections

Meet alumni and guest speakers who work in the industries you’re exploring.

Public Speaking

Practice introducing yourself and presenting an idea to a group.
