Before you go

What should I wear?

Check the dress code on each event announcement.

Business casual
Wear a collared shirt, sweater or blouse with dress pants, khakis or a skirt. A dress also works. Choose dress shoes or flats.
Business professional
Wear a matching pantsuit or skirt suit, a solid-color dress shirt and closed-toe dress shoes.
What if I have to miss an event?

Attend as many events as you can. If you have a conflict, let the VP of Recruitment know early. You can reach the chapter at @pctusf.

Can I also be in a social fraternity or sorority?

Yes. Members can participate in both PCT and social Greek life at USF.

How much are dues?

The chapter hasn’t published a current dollar amount. Ask at Info Night what members pay and what their dues cover.

How do I apply for membership?

Selected students receive an invitation to the final Rush event, where they can complete the membership application. If you receive a bid, the chapter is inviting you to join its pledge program and prepare for initiation.
