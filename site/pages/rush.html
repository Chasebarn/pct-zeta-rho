What Rush is

Rush is a series of events where prospective members meet the chapter. Info Night is a good place to start. Later events give you time to talk with alumni, try a professional workshop, and decide whether you want to apply.

Who can Rush

Academic path
USF students with a business or economics major or minor.
Class year
All class years, including first-year students and seniors.
Experience
You can participate without previous experience in a fraternity or professional organization.

What happens at Rush

Three events from the fall lineup give you a sense of what to expect.

Info Night
Meet members and ask what a semester in PCT looks like alongside your classes.
Professional Workshop
Try the professional development side of PCT. The chapter helps students build résumés, develop LinkedIn profiles, and practice networking.
Alumni Panel
Hear from members who have graduated. Bring a question about internships, choosing a field, or starting your first job.

What to wear

Check the event announcement for the dress code. The chapter gives two examples.

Business casual
A collared shirt, blouse, or sweater with dress pants, khakis, or a skirt. A dress also works. Choose dress shoes or flats.
Business professional
A matching suit with pants or a skirt, a solid-color dress shirt, and closed-toe dress shoes. Coordinate a tie or other accessories with your suit.

Common questions

Can I join if I’m in a social fraternity or sorority?

Yes. Zeta Rho has members who participate in both professional and social Greek life.

What if I have to miss an event?

Attend as many events as you can. Let the VP of Recruitment know as soon as you have a scheduling conflict. You can reach the chapter through @pctusf.

How much are dues?

Ask the recruitment team for this semester’s dues and any payment options at Info Night.

Is the interest form my application?

The interest form signs you up for recruitment updates. The chapter makes its membership application available at the invite-only event.

Selection and bids

If the chapter invites you to its final event, you can complete the membership application there.

A bid is an invitation to join PCT’s pledge program. Students who accept a bid prepare for possible initiation into the fraternity.
