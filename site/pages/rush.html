What Rush is

Rush introduces you to PCT’s members, culture, and expectations through a structured six-event process. You can ask questions, try professional activities, and get to know the people you would join.

The chapter gets to know you, too. The goal is to help both sides decide whether PCT is a good fit.

What happens at Rush

The chapter publishes the event lineup each recruitment cycle. The process gives you three ways to learn about PCT.

Get to know PCT
Learn how the chapter works, what members do during the semester, and what membership involves.
Meet the members
Speak with current members and chapter leadership. Ask about their experiences and the questions you have about joining.
Build professional skills
Practice communication and networking, with guidance on presenting your experience through a résumé and LinkedIn profile.

Common questions

Can I Rush with a business minor?

Yes. Business and economics majors and minors can participate.

Can I Rush as a first-year student or senior?

Yes. Rush is open to all class years.

Do I need professional or fraternity experience?

You can come to Rush without either. The process introduces the chapter’s expectations and gives you opportunities to learn.

What does membership cost?

Ask chapter leadership for current dues and any costs associated with recruitment or membership. Current amounts have not been published on the chapter’s Rush page.

Do I need to attend every event?

Ask the recruitment team about attendance expectations for the current cycle, especially if a class or work commitment overlaps with an event.

Does registering my interest commit me to joining?

The interest list lets you hear about recruitment. Rush is the opportunity for you and the chapter to get to know each other before membership decisions.

Selection and bids

Rush is a mutual fit process. Take part in the events, speak with members, and ask what the chapter expects from its members.

Chapter leadership communicates selection requirements, decisions, and any bid. The interest form records your interest in recruitment.
