Rush PCT

Meet the people behind Phi Chi Theta at USF and see what it’s like to be part of the chapter.

What Rush Is

Rush is a six-event introduction to Phi Chi Theta. You’ll meet members, learn what the chapter expects, and practice professional skills in a welcoming setting.

Who Can Rush

USF business and economics majors and minors can rush in any class year. No prior fraternity or professional experience is required.

What to Expect

Talk directly with members and chapter leadership. Ask how they spend their time in PCT, share what you’re looking for, and decide together whether the chapter is a good fit.

What You Gain

  • Résumé and LinkedIn

    Practice describing your experience in résumé bullets and on your LinkedIn profile.

  • Professional Connections

    Meet alumni, guest speakers, and people working in different industries.

  • Public Speaking and Communication

    Practice introducing yourself, speaking to a group, and sharing your ideas clearly.
