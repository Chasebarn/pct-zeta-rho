# V2 design and implementation requirements

- Match the chapter's palette, Instrument Serif and Inter, with an independently
  responsive frame. Keep the original V1 and legacy resources separate.
- Use the official crest with its original transparent artwork on the cream header.
  Keep the hero identity static; pair it with a real chapter portrait.
- Keep copy tied to what a prospective member can learn or do. No invented
  statistics, event dates, countdowns, dues, or schedule promises.
- The membership section has no overall visible heading. Its three buttons reveal
  the matching text and a single common photograph. Keyboard focus alone never
  changes the section or shifts the page.
- Keep two desktop / three phone company rows. Move with normal page scrolling,
  never pin the page. Fade a complete mark before clipping any of it. With reduced
  motion or JavaScript disabled, expose all 20 marks statically. Gold stats count
  once and preserve accessible final values.
- Maintain geographic accuracy: map only verified collegiate campuses, retain
  the NCES provenance and preserve each campus's exact position. Ordinary marks
  remain visible at all times. Keep the single subdued Tampa ring emphasis.
- Preserve the original cream photo arc without a title. The full-width purple
  Rush section overlaps the bottom. Preserve portrait subjects with wider crops,
  support keyboard and touch, and require user action to rotate the arc.
- Center the closing invitation and keep one brief CTA emphasis when in view.
  All links and essential content work without JavaScript.
- Verify reflow down to 320 CSS pixels, keyboard controls and focus, image and
  font loading, reduced motion, link destinations, and the actual hosted build.
