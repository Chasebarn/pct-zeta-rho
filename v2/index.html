Phi Chi Theta has operated as a national professional business fraternity since 1924. Membership connects Zeta Rho to alumni and chapters in cities across the country, which matters when members recruit, relocate, or need an introduction in a new market.

Zeta Rho was chartered at the University of South Florida in September 2018 by 18 founding business students.

How to join PCT.

Six events, open to business and economics majors and minors. Rush is free.

  1. 01

    Show up.

    Info Night is Wednesday, September 2 at 7:00 PM in BSN 225. You do not need prior experience or to know anyone there.

  2. 02

    Meet the chapter.

    Talk with members and exec in person. Bring the real questions: time commitment, dues, GPA, what the first semester looks like.

  3. 03

    Both sides decide.

    You are getting to know us while we get to know you. Nothing is settled on night one.

Phi Chi ThetaZeta Rho · USF

Phi Chi Theta’s Zeta Rho Chapter was established at the University of South Florida in September 2018 by 18 founding business students. Since its founding, the chapter has focused on preparing members for internships and full-time roles through structured professional development, service, and chapter standards. Today, PCT continues to grow as a professional organization that balances career outcomes, philanthropy, and an active chapter culture.
