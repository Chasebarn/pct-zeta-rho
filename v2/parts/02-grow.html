Graduate confident.

Not sure what comes after college? PCT is where you build the experience, confidence, and relationships that leave you better prepared for what comes after graduation.

Alumni, recruiters, and speakers who work in the fields you are aiming at. Fall Summit puts the chapter in a room with them for a full day, and members in other business majors become the people you ask before you apply.

Members run the work rather than attend it: chair an initiative, plan an event, manage a budget, make the call. Leadership becomes something you have already done before your first full-time job.

Formal, Spring Retreat, intramurals, and the traditions in between. The people who notice when you stop showing up, push you to apply, and are still around after graduation.
