A national fraternity since 1924.

Founded in Chicago, Phi Chi Theta is a coed professional fraternity for business and economics. Its mission centers on business education, professional leadership, and a local and national network for sharing ideas and resources.

Zeta Rho is the fraternity’s chapter at the University of South Florida. Members belong to a wider community of students and alumni beyond their own campus.

National programs include regional conferences, national mentoring, and stock market competitions. Scholarship opportunities through the Phi Chi Theta Educational Foundation recognize academic achievement, leadership, and commitment to the fraternity.
