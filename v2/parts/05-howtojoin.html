How to join PCT.

Six events, open to business and economics majors and minors. Rush is free.

  1. 01

    Show up.

    Info Night is Wednesday, September 2 at 7:00 PM in BSN 225. You do not need prior experience or to know anyone there.

  2. 02

    Meet the chapter.

    Talk with members and exec in person. Bring the real questions: time commitment, dues, GPA, what the first semester looks like.

  3. 03

    Both sides decide.

    You are getting to know us while we get to know you. Nothing is settled on night one.
